# Literature Summary 11: Kong et al. (2026), TabFM

**Citation:** Kong, W., Ilan, E. L., Nie, S., Narayan, T., Sen, R., Zhou, Y., Fu, D., Oymak, S., & Das, A. (2026). *TabFM: A zero-shot foundation model for tabular data* (arXiv:2609.37959). https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.37959

**Verified:** Yes (arXiv page, Google Research blog dated 30 June 2026, GitHub and Hugging Face model pages).
**Contribution:** 400M-parameter tabular foundation model trained on synthetic tables; in-context learning, zero-shot, single forward pass.
**Result:** First among default tabular foundation models on 51 TabArena datasets (38 classification, 13 regression); beats tuned AutoML pipelines.
**Limitation / gap:** Preprint (not yet peer reviewed); no medical data.
**Use in thesis:** Justifies TabFM as the second foundation model.
