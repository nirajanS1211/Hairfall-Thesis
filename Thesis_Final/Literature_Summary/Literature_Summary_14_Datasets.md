# Literature Summary 14: Datasets

## Mendeley: Hair Fall causes dataset — VERIFIED
Arnob, M. S., Islam, O., Tapos, M. A., & Chakraborty, N. R. (2024). *Dataset for evaluating hair fall causes using machine learning techniques* (Version 1) [Data set]. Mendeley Data. https://doi.org/10.17632/g46n66frrh.1
- Exists; published 24 June 2024; Daffodil International University; licence CC BY 4.0.
- 717 survey responses, 9 features (age, gender, hair product use, water quality, stress, late-night activity, anaemia, family history, and others), collected through Google Forms.
- The old proposal named it "Hair fall problem survey dataset (2023)" with no authors; the correct title and authors are above.
