# Literature Summary 2: Sai et al. (2023)

**Citation:** Sai, C. N. V., Archana, E., Vivek, B., Dhanwanth, B., & K. S., V. (2023). Enhancing hairfall prediction: A comparative analysis of individual algorithms and an ensemble method. *IJRITCC, 11*(6s), 499–508.

**Verified:** Yes (journal page found, ijritcc.org).
**Aim:** Compare single algorithms with an ensemble for hair fall prediction.
**Models:** SVM, KNN, decision tree, random forest, logistic regression, and an ensemble method.
**Result:** The ensemble outperformed every single algorithm in accuracy, precision, and recall.
**Limitation / gap:** No categorical-aware boosting, no explainability, dataset details not checked.
**Use in thesis:** Shows ensembles help on hair fall data, which supports testing boosting (CatBoost).
