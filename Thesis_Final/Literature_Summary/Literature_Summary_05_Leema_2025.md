# Literature Summary 5: Leema et al. (2025), HairSentinel

**Citation:** Leema, A. A., Saktheshwaran, T., Sri, G. R., & Balakrishnan, P. (2025). HairSentinel: A time-aware anomaly detection framework for forecasting hairfall trends using temporal fusion transformers. *Frontiers in Artificial Intelligence, 8*. https://doi.org/10.3389/frai.2025.1649740

**Verified:** Yes (Frontiers article read).
**Data:** Real-time questionnaire data from 750 participants, collected February–March 2025; not public.
**Models:** LSTM, Random Forest, Temporal Fusion Transformer (TFT), ARIMAX.
**Result:** TFT reached 97.5% accuracy and 97.4% precision for anomaly detection.
**Limitation / gap:** Private dataset, self-reported data, limited generalisation.
**Use in thesis:** A transformer-based approach on hair fall data; shows interest in transformers but on time series.
