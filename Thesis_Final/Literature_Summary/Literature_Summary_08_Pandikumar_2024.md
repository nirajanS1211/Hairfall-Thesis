# Literature Summary 8: Pandikumar et al. (2024)

**Citation:** Pandikumar, S., Sevugapandi, N., & Arun, M. (2024). Predicting hair loss with AI: A deep learning framework combining genetic and scalp health data. In *Integrating AI, machine learning, and IoT in bioinformatics* (pp. 100–107). QTanalytics. https://doi.org/10.48001/978-81-966500-0-1-6

**Verified:** Yes. NOTE: the old proposal cited this as "Chen, Zhang & Wang (2024)", CRC Press. Those authors and that publisher do not match; the real authors are above.
**Method:** CNN on high-resolution scalp images plus LSTM on lifestyle and health sequences.
**Result:** A framework is proposed; the chapter is short (8 pages).
**Limitation / gap:** Needs images; limited evidence of performance.
**Use in thesis:** Deep learning example that combines genetic, hormonal, and lifestyle data.
