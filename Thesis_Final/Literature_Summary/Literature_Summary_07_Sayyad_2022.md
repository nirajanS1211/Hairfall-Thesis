# Literature Summary 7: Sayyad et al. (2022)

**Citation:** Sayyad, S., Midhunchakkaravarthy, D., & Sayyad, F. (2022). An analysis of alopecia areata classification framework for human hair loss based on VGG-SVM approach. *Journal of Pharmaceutical Negative Results, 13*(Suppl. 1), 9–15. https://doi.org/10.47750/pnr.2022.13.s01.02

**Verified:** Yes. NOTE: the old proposal cited "Sayyad & Midhunchakkaravarthy (2023), Journal of Dermatological Informatics, 15(2), 45–52". That journal article could not be found; this 2022 paper is the real one (a related review exists on SSRN, 2022).
**Data:** 200 healthy-hair images (Figaro1k) and 68 alopecia areata images (DermNet).
**Method:** VGG network with SVM classifier.
**Result:** 98.31% accuracy.
**Limitation / gap:** Small image set; one condition.
**Use in thesis:** Image-based deep learning example.
