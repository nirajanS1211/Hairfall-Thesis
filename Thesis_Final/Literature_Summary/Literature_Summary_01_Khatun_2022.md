# Literature Summary 1: Khatun et al. (2022)

**Citation:** Khatun, M., Ajmain, M., Khushbu, S. A., Ria, N. J., & Noori, S. R. H. (2022). Survey-based machine learning approaches to diagnosis of hair fall disorder in Bangladeshi community. *2022 ICCCNT*, 1–6. https://doi.org/10.1109/ICCCNT54827.2022.9984226

**Verified:** Yes (Crossref record and conference listing found).
**Aim:** Diagnose hair fall disorder from questionnaire answers.
**Data:** Survey of 610 participants in Bangladesh, mostly aged 18–24 (about 57%), 55.9% female.
**Models:** SVM, KNN, Logistic Regression, Random Forest, XGBoost.
**Result:** XGBoost was best with 92.62% accuracy. Reported causes: stress, allergies, dandruff, family history.
**Limitation / gap:** Only classical models; no explanation of predictions; no foundation models.
**Use in thesis:** Evidence that survey data can predict hair fall; shows real-world burden in young adults.
