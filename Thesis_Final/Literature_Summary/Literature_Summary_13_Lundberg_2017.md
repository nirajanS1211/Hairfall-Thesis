# Literature Summary 13: Lundberg and Lee (2017), SHAP

**Citation:** Lundberg, S. M., & Lee, S.-I. (2017). A unified approach to interpreting model predictions. *NeurIPS, 30*, 4765–4774.

**Verified:** Yes (well-known NeurIPS 2017 paper).
**Contribution:** SHAP assigns each feature a Shapley-value contribution to a prediction; model-agnostic.
**Limitation / gap:** General method, not a hair loss study.
**Use in thesis:** Basis for the explainability layer that is applied to all three models.
**Correction:** the old proposal cited this in "the broader clinical machine learning literature stresses accuracy alone is insufficient". It is the SHAP method paper, so that claim was removed.
