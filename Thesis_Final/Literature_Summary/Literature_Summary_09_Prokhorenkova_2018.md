# Literature Summary 9: Prokhorenkova et al. (2018), CatBoost

**Citation:** Prokhorenkova, L., Gusev, G., Vorobev, A., Dorogush, A. V., & Gulin, A. (2018). CatBoost: Unbiased boosting with categorical features. *NeurIPS, 31*, 6638–6648.

**Verified:** Yes (well-known NeurIPS 2018 paper).
**Contribution:** Ordered boosting and ordered target statistics that remove target leakage when encoding categorical features.
**Result:** Outperformed other boosting libraries on benchmark datasets.
**Limitation / gap:** Not a health study.
**Use in thesis:** Justifies CatBoost as the boosted-tree baseline for survey-style categorical data.
**Correction:** the old proposal wrongly cited this paper for "dermatological studies showing CatBoost wins". It is the CatBoost method paper only.
