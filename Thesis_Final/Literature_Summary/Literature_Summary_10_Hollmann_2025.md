# Literature Summary 10: Hollmann et al. (2025), TabPFN

**Citation:** Hollmann, N., Müller, S., Purucker, L., Krishnakumar, A., Körfer, M., Hoo, S. B., Schirrmeister, R. T., & Hutter, F. (2025). Accurate predictions on small data with a tabular foundation model. *Nature, 637*(8045), 319–326. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-024-08328-6

**Verified:** Yes.
**Contribution:** TabPFN, a transformer pretrained on synthetic datasets; predicts in one forward pass without training; best on tables under about 10,000 rows.
**Limitation / gap:** Not tested on hair loss or health surveys.
**Use in thesis:** Justifies TabPFN as the first foundation model.
**Correction:** the old proposal cited "Hollmann et al. (2023), Nature Machine Intelligence, 5(5)". That journal and volume are wrong. The 2023 version is an ICLR paper ("TabPFN: A transformer that solves small tabular classification problems in a second"); the Nature paper above is the newer, published version.
