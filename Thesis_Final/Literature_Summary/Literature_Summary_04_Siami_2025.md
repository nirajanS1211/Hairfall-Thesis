# Literature Summary 4: Siami and Azis (2025)

**Citation:** Siami, M. I., & Azis, H. (2025). Predicting hair loss with machine learning: A multi-factor analysis. *International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Medical Issues, 3*(1). https://doi.org/10.56705/ijaimi.v3i1.360

**Verified:** Yes (journal page found).
**Data:** Balanced dataset of genetic, hormonal, lifestyle, and environmental indicators.
**Models:** Logistic Regression, Decision Tree, Random Forest, Gradient Boosting, XGBoost, voting ensemble.
**Result:** Accuracy and F1 only about 50% at best; age, stress, and nutritional deficiency were the prominent factors.
**Limitation / gap:** Weak signal; authors say richer clinical inputs are needed.
**Use in thesis:** Motivates using clinical blood-test features together with lifestyle features.
